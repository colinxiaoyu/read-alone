Read Alone uses locally generated synthetic speech.

English: Piper, en_GB-cori-high (British English female).
Voice model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_GB/cori/high
Piper voice repository license: MIT.
Dataset: approximately 24 hours of LibriVox recordings, credited in the Cori model card.
https://librivox.org
Dataset license stated in the model card: public domain.
Generation tool: Piper, Open Home Foundation and contributors.
https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl — GPL-3.0.

Mandarin Chinese: Kokoro-82M v1.1-zh, preset zf_001.
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh
Model license: Apache License 2.0.
Model trained by rzvzn, based on StyleTTS2 and ISTFTNet;
Chinese training data provided by LongMaoData, as credited in the model card.
ONNX wrapper: kokoro-onnx, thewh1teagle and contributors, MIT.
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
Chinese grapheme-to-phoneme processing: Misaki, hexgrad and contributors, Apache-2.0.
https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki

English phonemization uses eSpeak NG through Piper.
These generation tools are separate from the static playback app.

Reference audio for pace and pause analysis:
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MRZFq23KBw8
The reference source has its own rights and is separate from model licenses.
